# Safety and under-13 policy

**Current minimum age: 13.** Enforced in `/api/auth/register` using DOB-derived age band; the raw date of birth is not stored. Teen accounts default to private, and messaging is disabled. The 10–12 experience is **not available**. Releasing it requires compliance work and actual verified parental consent, rather than a checkbox.

User-generated content is the main risk: implement proactive content scanning, moderation staffing, reporting and blocking, removal/suspension workflows, incident response, image/video abuse reporting, safety audits, privacy policy, deletion/export requests, and escalation channels before launch. Live video and private messages need additional controls and are intentionally not implemented in v0.1.

Non-compliant content can include personal information, harassment, sexualized content, exploitation, hate, and harmful challenges. Policies and enforcement must be appropriate for school-aged creators and viewers.

**Do not** treat a claimed App Store age rating as proof of legal or platform compliance. Consult qualified privacy/legal counsel before launching to children.
